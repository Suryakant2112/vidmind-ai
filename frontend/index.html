Paste a YouTube link. You get a short summary, proper study notes and a place to ask questions. Every point links back to the minute it was said.

On YouTube, open the description, click Show transcript, select all of it and paste it below. Subtitle files (.srt or .vtt) work too. Keep the link above filled in so the video and timestamps still work.
